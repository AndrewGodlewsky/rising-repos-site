Set large losses aside

A step from one count to the next that lost more than 150 stars, or 3% of its stars if that is more, is taken as bad data and left out of both paces. A gain of any size always counts.

Are enough days left?

At least 1 of the 2 recent days, and 7 of the 12 before them.

3 · Is it rising?

Work out its score

recent pace
stars in the 2 recent days ÷ 2
usual pace
stars in the 12 days before ÷ 12
score
recent pace ÷ usual pace, taken as at least 1

Each pace divides by the days the rule on losses kept. Taking the usual pace as at least 1 star a day, the floor, keeps a quiet repository from scoring a huge multiple of almost nothing.

Is the score 3 or more?

Stars coming in at three times its usual pace or faster. Steady growth scores about 1, however fast it is.

How far it is above its own trend is the stars the two recent days brought beyond its usual pace: (recent pace − usual pace) × 2. How far it needs to be depends on its stars on the board day:

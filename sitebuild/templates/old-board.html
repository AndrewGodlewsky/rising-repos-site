{# The notice of an old board (AndrewGodlewsky/RisingRepos#56), and its script, old-board.js. As built, the notice is hidden and holds no words, so that with scripts turned off the page is the fresh page. It carries the newest board's day, written by the same filter as every other date on the page, and its publish time, whichever board the page shows. The script measures the board's age from that time by the reader's clock, and writes the day as it finds it. Below 26 hours, with the clock set behind the publish time, or with a time not in the exact shape written here, it changes nothing. From 26 hours it fills the notice and shows it. From 7 days it also sets data-withheld on the root element, and the stylesheet hides what
holds: the board, or the list of board days. It writes its own words, the day as the build wrote it, and the numbers it works out, only as text, and reads nothing else from the page. The notice comes right after the site's line, before the date, the strip, and the main content. Its script is part of the page's one script, in the head (site.js), which fills it the moment it is parsed, before any of those can be drawn, so a notice it shows moves nothing already on the screen. #}

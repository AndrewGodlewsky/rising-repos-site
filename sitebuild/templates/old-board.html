{# The notice of an old board (AndrewGodlewsky/RisingRepos#56), and its script, old-board.js. As built, the notice is hidden and holds no words, so that with scripts turned off the page is the fresh page. It carries the newest board's day and publish time, whichever board the page shows. The script reads those two and measures the board's age by the reader's clock. Below 26 hours, with the clock set behind the publish time, or with a day or a time not in the exact shape written here, it changes nothing. From 26 hours it fills the notice and shows it. From 7 days it also sets data-withheld on the root element, and the stylesheet hides what
holds: the board, or the list of board days. It writes its own words and the numbers it works out, only as text, and reads nothing else from the page. The two come right after the site's line, before the date, the strip, and the main content, so the script runs before any of those is parsed, and a notice it shows moves nothing already on the screen. page.html lets the script run by the hash of its text, which the build works out. #}
